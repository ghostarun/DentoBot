## 17:50 IST — Step 5C dentist-directed shell finalization and export boundary

### Request and design

- The developer requested moving STL export from Step 5B to Step 5C and adding
  a simple dentist-facing shell-height trim before export. The requested
  interaction isolates the shell in a front view, lets the dentist choose a
  horizontal cut, and retains advanced Dynamic Modeler options. The important
  manual-workflow alternative is an adjustable closed dotted margin around the
  uneven base of the teeth rather than one flat plane.
- Kept the Step 5B shell immutable as raw derived geometry. Step 5C owns a
  separate finalized shell and is the only export boundary. This preserves
  retries and provenance and prevents an untrimmed shell from being treated as
  export-ready.
- Chose Slicer's built-in Markups plus Dynamic Modeler. Plane mode uses capped
  positive/negative Plane Cut. Curve mode uses a surface-snapped closed curve,
  inside/outside Curve Cut, then explicit capping, triangulation, cleanup,
  normals, and watertightness validation.
- The interaction view is an anterior parallel world-RAS camera: screen
  horizontal is R/L and vertical is S/I. Its optional lock constrains camera
  orientation and the trim-plane normal but permits zoom and height
  translation. This is deliberately not called a dental/occlusal frame, and
  the suggested 70–80 percent tooth coverage is not automated.

### Implementation

- Added Step 5C parameter state, role/reference contracts, lineage tags,
  source-revision and edit-geometry fingerprints, stale detection, isolated
  visibility/camera state, plane and curve markup placement, Dynamic Modeler
  execution, finalized-shell topology metrics, owned reset, and Step 5B-to-5C
  cascade cleanup in `DENTOWorkflow/DENTOWorkflow.py`.
- Added the Step 5C UI and moved **Export Final Shell + Sleeve STL** out of Step
  5B in `DENTOWorkflow/Resources/UI/DENTOWorkflow.ui`. The panel offers
  plane/curve modes, kept side/region, view lock, view isolation/restoration,
  markup creation, preview/apply, expert Dynamic Modeler handoff, reset, and
  export.
- Declared built-in `DynamicModeler` and `Markups` dependencies. Export now
  rejects the raw Step 5B shell, stale/mismatched finalized output, missing
  source provenance, non-watertight topology, and a stale sleeve. The final
  shell is saved separately and remains reconstructible after MRB reload.
