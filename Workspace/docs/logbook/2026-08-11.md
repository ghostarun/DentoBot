## Queued follow-up — semi-automatic support-boundary initialization

- After live acceptance of the current undercut/terminal-latch work, implement
  a two-stage initializer: a trajectory-frame plane with locked orientation
  and operator-controlled coronal/apical height, followed by an automatically
  generated but fully editable closed Markups boundary.
- The planned orientation study will compare a robust local crown-cap/
  occlusal-plane fit across selected teeth against a target-tooth anatomical
  axis derived from crown and centerline/furcation or root-centroid landmarks.
  A single cusp tangent, world-horizontal plane, or whole-tooth PCA alone is
  not accepted as sufficiently stable for multi-root teeth.
- Plane cutting produces separate loops on disconnected CBCT tooth surfaces.
  The queued geometry stage therefore explicitly includes contour selection
  and a controlled planar outer-envelope/gap-bridge operation before creating
  the existing editable world-RAS closed curve.
- Standard CBCT cannot be assumed to show the true gingival margin reliably.
  Registered intraoral/desktop scans are the preferred future source for
  visible crown and gingival geometry. CBCT-only CEJ/cervical constriction or
  alveolar-crest offsets may be evaluated only as labelled initialization
  proxies followed by clinician correction. No code, scene, export, Git, or
  Drive operation was performed for this queued design item.

## Directional undercut removal and partial terminal-tooth latches

### Report and diagnosis

- The representative shell retained filler between teeth and at outer corners,
  so its apparent snugness did not imply that it could seat and be removed.
  The normal-based red undercut surface was only a diagnostic; the directional
  height-field blockout received selected support anatomy alone and could not
  see adjacent unselected teeth.
- Inspection reconfirmed that SlicerFSP `RegisterModule.py` classifies normals
  against hardcoded world axes, hollows the selected undercut surface, and
  unions it through global Segment Editor state. It provides useful blockout
  concepts but not a complete insertion-frame automatic solver suitable for
  DENTOBOT's disconnected CBCT tooth segments and explicit MRML contracts.
- The reference guide also established a separate terminal-shape requirement:
  the guide should finish on the inward portion of its two outer supports,
  rather than end abruptly or extend toward adjacent unselected teeth.

### Implementation

- Added substantive largest-island extraction per authoritative tooth and used
  same-arch tooth surfaces as collision-only anatomy. They influence removal
  clearance but remain excluded from the clinician-selected contact preview.
- Extended the cropped insertion-frame height field with configurable
  transverse-only interproximal relief (`1.0 mm` research default). It closes
  narrow embrasures perpendicular to insertion without closing along the
  insertion/removal axis.
- Added configurable terminal support coverage (`50%` research default) to
  Step 5A. Mapped tooth-patch centroids are projected perpendicular to the
  trajectory-derived insertion axis; PCA finds the local support span; each
  non-target endpoint is clipped toward the group. A target at an endpoint is
  preserved for edge-molar cases, and fewer than three mapped teeth are left
  unchanged rather than using an unstable automatic plane.
- Persisted terminal planes in world RAS and reapplied them after boundary
  bridge union, clearance subtraction, and voxel closing. Thus the orange
  preview, fitting surface, structural bridge, and final shell use the same
  terminal envelope. Changing coverage invalidates all descendants.
- Retained the single-voxel contour-speckle filter from the preceding shell
  repair while continuing to reject substantive disconnected geometry.

## Trajectory-directed side selection and tooth/island accounting

### Report and design

- Representative anatomy showed that a single Smaller/Larger toggle cannot
  retain crown-side patches on unequal teeth: the desired patch can be the
  smaller candidate on one tooth and the larger candidate on another.
- The developer established Entry→Target on the target-tooth trajectory as the
  default crown-to-root insertion vector. The opposite is the crown/removal
  direction applied to every support tooth. A compact optional polarity
  reversal is the only routine override.
- The same scene reported three of six “tooth surfaces” despite exactly one
  target and two selected supports. Diagnosis found that raw connected mesh
  islands were being counted as teeth after the source segment identities had
  been lost in the combined draft model.

### Implementation

- Step 5A now resolves and validates the currently selected complete trajectory
  against the draft model's authoritative segmentation and target segment.
  Both Dijkstra clip candidates are generated per addressed tooth and scored
  by area-weighted displacement along the crown/removal direction. Candidate
  area is recorded but does not decide the retained side.
- Replaced the clinical Smaller/Larger dropdown with an explanatory automatic
  direction label and an off-by-default **Reverse polarity** button. Changing
  trajectory points, selected trajectory, or polarity invalidates the preview
  and descendants.
- Preview extraction receives separate world-RAS surfaces keyed by stable
  segment ID. Boundary samples are assigned to teeth first, then to the most
  strongly addressed connected island inside that tooth. Status/metrics report
  selected/omitted teeth separately from ignored internal mesh islands.
- Preview provenance now references the source trajectory and stores its
  direction/polarity geometry. Generation also creates a locked, non-selectable
  derived Approach→Seat line for existing undercut/blockout code; no extra
  manual direction placement is required in the routine path.

### Verification and remaining acceptance

- A new headless Slicer geometry regression passed with unequal synthetic tooth
  sizes where crown selection intentionally required a mix of Smaller and
  Larger candidates. Three authoritative tooth IDs containing six raw mesh
  islands were reported as three selected teeth plus three ignored diagnostic
  islands. Reversing polarity selected the opposite root-side candidates.
- The full focused visible-support vertical slice passed trajectory-derived
  direction, transformed world-RAS geometry, downstream undercut/shell/fusion,
  MRB save/reload of trajectory/derived-line references, and clean deletion.
- Registered widget loading passed with the Reverse polarity control and the
  derived direction selector read-only. The active trajectory-selection widget

- Representative-anatomy acceptance remains required. Save and restart/reload
