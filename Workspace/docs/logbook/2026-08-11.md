  visible-support preview and the interface did not explain the required
  interaction clearly.

  loop to provide a valid Dijkstra boundary for every disconnected tooth in
  the draft model. One untouched or unmappable tooth therefore aborted valid

  inspect/redraw when an intended support surface was omitted.

## Trajectory-directed side selection and tooth/island accounting

### Report and design

- Representative anatomy showed that a single Smaller/Larger toggle cannot
  retain crown-side patches on unequal teeth: the desired patch can be the
  smaller candidate on one tooth and the larger candidate on another.
- The developer established Entry→Target on the target-tooth trajectory as the
  default crown-to-root insertion vector. The opposite is the crown/removal
  direction applied to every support tooth. A compact optional polarity
  reversal is the only routine override.
- The same scene reported three of six “tooth surfaces” despite exactly one
  target and two selected supports. Diagnosis found that raw connected mesh
  islands were being counted as teeth after the source segment identities had
  been lost in the combined draft model.

### Implementation

- Step 5A now resolves and validates the currently selected complete trajectory
  against the draft model's authoritative segmentation and target segment.
  Both Dijkstra clip candidates are generated per addressed tooth and scored
  by area-weighted displacement along the crown/removal direction. Candidate
  area is recorded but does not decide the retained side.
- Replaced the clinical Smaller/Larger dropdown with an explanatory automatic
  direction label and an off-by-default **Reverse polarity** button. Changing
  trajectory points, selected trajectory, or polarity invalidates the preview
  and descendants.
- Preview extraction receives separate world-RAS surfaces keyed by stable
  segment ID. Boundary samples are assigned to teeth first, then to the most
  strongly addressed connected island inside that tooth. Status/metrics report
  selected/omitted teeth separately from ignored internal mesh islands.
- Preview provenance now references the source trajectory and stores its
  direction/polarity geometry. Generation also creates a locked, non-selectable
  derived Approach→Seat line for existing undercut/blockout code; no extra
  manual direction placement is required in the routine path.

### Verification and remaining acceptance

- A new headless Slicer geometry regression passed with unequal synthetic tooth
  sizes where crown selection intentionally required a mix of Smaller and
  Larger candidates. Three authoritative tooth IDs containing six raw mesh
  islands were reported as three selected teeth plus three ignored diagnostic

- The full focused visible-support vertical slice passed trajectory-derived
  direction, transformed world-RAS geometry, downstream undercut/shell/fusion,
  MRB save/reload of trajectory/derived-line references, and clean deletion.
- Registered widget loading passed with the Reverse polarity control and the
  derived direction selector read-only. The active trajectory-selection widget

- Representative-anatomy acceptance remains required. Save and restart/reload

  scene close. Segmentation data and workflow references are unchanged.

  boundary/non-manifold edges. Inspection of the installed Slicer 5.10 source
