### Engineering interpretation

The earlier model let an orientation solve use a free spindle roll and then
canonicalize that result to J6 zero. That can make a pose appear reachable even
though the robot cannot command the roll. The minimal truthful boundary is a
five-variable `dentobot_arm` ending at a fixed sibling TCP at the CAD burr-tip
reference pose; the downstream spinning branch may remain for visualization
and collision geometry.
