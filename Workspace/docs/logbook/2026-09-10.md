### Ownership conclusion

CBCT/anatomy is case-shared. Target identity is case-target state referenced by
the branch. Trajectories, explicit pairing intent, patient shell, unified
template, guides and Step 5C evidence revision are PreparedBranch-owned. Step
4B support/Step 5A visible surface is reusable case-target-derived state only
under exact dependency equality. Step 4C docking and insertion direction are
branch-dependent upstream state, never case-shared by storage accident. Jaw
opening and landmarks/base/Home are reusable shared runtime setup only while
their inputs remain unchanged; saved configuration is not live validity.
Collision acknowledgement, plans, previews, guards and diagnostics are branch-
runtime state and invalidate on branch switch.
