### 16:05 IST — Intraoral volume at a fixed incisor opening

- Stated goal: approximate intraoral space volume at a controlled
  upper–lower incisor gap (same class of quantity as the Step 6 40 mm
  opening), not a fused cosmetic mesh.
- Densest frame `0000172.obj`: 358,865 points, AABB ~121 × 93 × 110 mm
  (1.24 L box), PCA thickness only ~8 mm RMS, mean RGB (0.59, 0.23, 0.23)
  with 98% reddish vertices. Occupied 2 mm voxels are a ~47 mL *surface
  shell*, not a cavity. This looks like extraoral skin/lip surface.
- Implication: this zip cannot support a defensible intraoral cavity
  volume. A volume number requires named caps (palate, tongue or retracted
  tongue plane, buccal walls, lip aperture, posterior plane) plus an
  independently measured incisor gap. iPhone LiDAR can check the extraoral
  opening; internal envelope needs IOS/CBCT or an explicit geometric
  assumption.

### 16:10 IST — Extraoral opening and incisor gap capture

- Intraoral cavity volume is out of scope. The needed quantity is extraoral
  mouth opening plus incisor gap at a chosen opening, likely the same 40 mm
  class as Step 6.
- Current zip is the wrong capture for that: a walking camera-frame sequence
  of mostly lip/skin surface, with no scale object and no guaranteed view of
  both incisal edges.
- Capture advice recorded for the next dataset: physical spacer or calipers
  own the gap; retract lips; one frontal still plus one sagittal still;
  include the spacer as a visible scale; do not use a 195-frame walkaround.
  Phone LiDAR remains a centimetre-level aperture check, not the millimetre
  authority for incisor distance.
