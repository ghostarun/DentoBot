## Step 4C independent occlusal-plane dock correction

### Clarified geometry contract

- The robot-facing top/opening of every Step 4C dock must lie on the fitted
  target-crown occlusal plane. The plane is not the tooth-side base of an
  outward extrusion and is not permission to create a solid crown-centred hub.
- The four robot/registration docks, their shell attachments, and the annular
  trajectory drill-guide sleeve/local collar are three distinct geometry
  roles. The rejected hub/spokes had conflated them and could obscure the
  drill-guide region.

### Implementation

- Updated `compute_target_docking_frame()` so Entry→Target establishes only
  crown/root polarity. A target-crown-cap PCA normal now defines the occlusal
  plane, with a trajectory-perpendicular fallback for a degenerate or
  greater-than-60-degree fit.
- Updated `create_target_frame_docking_geometry()` to generate four independent
  hollow docks, no central hub, and no radial spokes. Every theoretical top
  face has zero residual from the stored plane and dock depth proceeds along
  the crown-to-root occlusal normal.
- Reinterpreted the existing visible connector controls as shell-attachment
  branch width and endpoint overlap. Step 5B now creates four separate
  closest-surface dock-to-shell branches instead of one bridge from a combined
  crown-centred assembly.
- Added trajectory-guide exclusion processing. Attachment/reinforcement
  geometry is clipped against the protected guide clearance; a core robot-dock
  intersection is a hard generation error rather than silently trimming the
  dock and changing its mechanical dimensions.
- Kept the annular trajectory primitive but renamed its methods/UI descriptions
  as a drill-guide sleeve and local collar rather than a robot dock.
- Bumped the Step 4C geometry schema from `1.0` to `2.0`; older hub-layout
  scenes remain deletable but report Stale and require explicit regeneration.
- Expanded Step 5C verification to require four independent components, no
  central hub/spokes, four shell attachments, the guide-exclusion record, and
  zero core-dock/exclusion overlap.
